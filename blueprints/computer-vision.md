# Computer Vision specialization blueprint

I can start this track after the common foundation and PyTorch. I will confirm the choice with my supervisor. The guide estimates 12–16 weeks.

## Required course sequence

1. [Stanford CS231n](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/): 6–8 weeks. Classification, linear classifiers, optimization, regularization, backprop, CNNs, transfer learning, attention, ViT, detection, segmentation, self-supervision, diffusion and vision-language models. Use permitted independent implementations and extensions.
2. [Hugging Face CV](https://huggingface.co/learn/computer-vision-course/unit0/welcome/welcome): 4–6 weeks. Guide-selected units 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 11, 12: CNNs, ViT, multimodal/generative models, classification/detection/segmentation, optimization, zero-shot vision and ethics.
3. Optional selected [fast.ai](https://course.fast.ai/) lessons: getting started, deployment, convolutions and selected diffusion/attention lessons.

## Repository modules

`01-cs231n-foundations/`, `02-modern-cv/`, `03-optimization-ethics/`, and optional `04-fastai/`. Include original implementations, concept notes, training logs, ViT or multimodal work, detection or segmentation, and appropriate metrics.
